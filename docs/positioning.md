# AtLeast — Positioning Guide

The short answer to "who is this for, what is it, and why is it better." Use it
as the source of truth for marketing copy, article intros, and App Store text.
Every claim here is verifiable on the live site.

---

## 1. Who it's for

People who practise on a timer but don't want to watch one. Meditators sitting
with their eyes closed. Breathwork practitioners pacing box breathing, 4-7-8, or
Wim Hof rounds. Anyone in a cold plunge with wet hands and no interest in
reaching for a phone. Yoga and mobility practitioners giving each side an equal
hold. Lifters counting out a plank or a wall sit. Knowledge workers who want a
focus block that ends without an alarm announcing itself to the room.

What they have in common is not the practice — it's the problem. They need to
know time is passing without breaking the state they're in. A conventional timer
solves that with an interruption: a screen to check, or an alarm that arrives
exactly when concentration is deepest.

They already own an Apple Watch and would rather the timing happened there.
Many are privacy-conscious enough to notice when a wellness app asks for an
account before it will count to ten. They are practitioners, not consumers of
wellness content, and they respond to a calm tone rather than a loud one.

## 2. What it is

*AtLeast* is a passive haptic timer for Apple Watch. You set a minimum duration
(1–60 minutes) and a tap interval. Gentle taps on your wrist mark time during the
session. When the taps stop, you have reached your minimum. There is no alarm, no
sound, and no screen to check.

The framing is "set a floor, not a ceiling." Instead of counting down to an
interruption, you commit to *at least* a few minutes and let a quiet rhythm carry
you there. Silence is the completion signal.

It runs entirely on the Apple Watch, works offline with no phone nearby, and
continues its haptics with the watch face off. An optional iPhone companion lets
you pre-load or pick a timer on the larger screen and mirror the live session;
the taps always happen on the wrist. It requires Apple Watch Series 6 or later
running watchOS 26.0 or later.

*AtLeast* is privacy-first: no accounts, session and haptic logic on-device, and
cookieless Plausible analytics only — never for advertising or cross-app
tracking. It is currently a free TestFlight beta.

## 3. Why it's better

**The end of the session doesn't interrupt it.** Traditional timers break the
state they were meant to protect. *AtLeast* inverts that: taps encourage you
during the practice and go silent once you've earned it. The absence of a signal
is the signal.

**It's eyes-free by design.** Built for the wrist, not adapted from a phone.
Nothing to look at, nothing to unlock, nothing to dry off first. That matters
most exactly where phones fail — eyes closed, hands cold, room dark, shared space.

**A minimum is more forgiving than a deadline.** Committing to *at least* five
minutes is easier to keep than promising exactly twenty, and it leaves room to
continue rather than forcing a stop.

**It does one thing.** No account, no feed, no streak, no library of guided
content to subscribe to. Set a duration and an interval, press Start on the
Watch, and put your attention where it belongs.

**Privacy is a genuine differentiator, not a badge.** Session and haptic logic
run on-device, there is nothing to sign in to, and analytics are cookieless and
never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
