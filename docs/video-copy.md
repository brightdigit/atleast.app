# Atleast Demo Video
To me these always something missing from Timer apps. They are great when you need to do something at a certain time or finish something in a certain amount of time. 
However some things you want to keep going, you don’t want something to loudly tell you to stop but just gently let you know when you’ve met that goal, that minimum amount you had to do that day. Whether it’s yoga, meditation, prayer time, rows, push up whatever.
As a father of 6 children and an indie software developer, it’s important for me to take some quiet time for at least 15 minutes per day.
And the last thing you are going to want to do is have to tap your watch to stop that blaring alarm.
That’s why I built AtLeast - AtLeast is a passive timer that uses gentle taps on the Apple Watch to let you know to keep going. When you stop feeling those taps you know you’ve reached your minimum time. It doesn’t interupt your flow - it let’s you keep going.
