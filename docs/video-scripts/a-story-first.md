Shot codes and the ending are explained in the [README](README.md). Shot IDs (`W3`, `P5`…) refer to the [shot list](shot-list.md).

## Watch footage: Device Hub recordings

The Watch screens (W1–W5) were **screen-recorded with Device Hub**, alongside the iPhone recordings, instead of filmed in macro. So in this version `SR` covers both devices:

- **SR W…** means the Device Hub recording of the Watch, and **SR P…** the iPhone recording. It's still the app running on the real device, which is what the contest rules ask for.
- **Frame the Watch recording.** It's small and square, so scale it up on the brand background (`#0A0A0A`) or place it in an Apple Watch bezel from Apple's marketing resources. Use the same treatment every time it appears.
- **A recording can't show the wrist or the motor.** The pulse beats (1:02, 1:08) need the physical feel, so lay the W3 recording over L4 (wrist on the knee, eyes closed) or cut between them. Record the motor buzz and room tone separately (NAT) and sync them to the pulse.
- **D1 stays one camera shot** of the phone and Watch together. Don't build the handoff as a split screen of the two recordings, because that's the one place a recording can't prove it's real.

## Captions and graphics

The timeline is **3840×2160 at 23.976 fps** (the same as the `Story-A-TwoMin.mov` rough cut). All sizes and positions below are pixels on that frame, with (0, 0) at the top left. The **FCP position** column is the same point as FCP's Transform Position, which is measured from the centre of the frame: X = px − 1920, Y = 1080 − px. Graphic IDs (`G1`…`G5`) refer to the [shot list](shot-list.md#graphics).

### Shared style

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Font | **SF Pro Display**, the system font the site uses. No other typefaces |
| Colors | Text `#F5F5F5`. Accent `#6B8FF8` (step numbers, "AtLeast Pro", the lower-third bar). Secondary text `#F5F5F5` at 70% opacity. Background `#0A0A0A` |
| Case | Sentence case, as written in the script. No all-caps, and no full stop except on **Silence means enough.** |
| Safe area | Keep all text at least **288 px from the left and right** and **216 px from the top and bottom**. Nothing goes below y = 1900, so YouTube's controls and progress bar never cover it |
| Legibility | Over camera footage, add a soft shadow: black, 40% opacity, blur 24 px, no offset. Over the `#0A0A0A` background, no shadow. No boxes, pills or scrims |
| Motion | **Fade in and out only**: 8 frames in, 8 frames out. No slides, wipes, typewriter or bounce. A caption that changes in place (step 1 → 2 → 3) cross-fades over 8 frames |
| Timing | Bring a caption in on the word it matches, not before. Hold it for the whole beat, and at least 2 seconds |

### Layout: device on the right, text on the left

Every beat that shows a screen recording uses one layout, so the eye learns where to look:

- **The device sits right of centre**, centred on x = 2496 (FCP X = +576, Y = 0).
  - **Watch (W1–W5):** scale the Device Hub recording, in its bezel, to about **1500 px tall**. Use this size and position every time.
  - **iPhone (P1–P8):** scale the recording to about **1880 px tall** (about 865 px wide), on `#0A0A0A`. This also fixes the flat white panel in the rough cut.
- **Captions sit in a left column**, left-aligned at **x = 288** (FCP X = −1632), no wider than **1500 px**, so they never touch the device. They're vertically centred on y = 1080 unless noted.
- Where the W3 recording is laid over L4 (the wrist on the knee), keep the Watch recording at the same size and place; the camera shot fills the frame behind it.

### Checking the captions

- Watch the export on a phone, where it's smallest. If a 72 px line is hard to read there, raise everything by the same factor rather than one caption at a time.
- Check that no caption sits on top of the Watch or iPhone recording, and that none is on screen during a straight cut it doesn't belong to.
- In FCP, some title templates show their size relative to 1080p. If 96 looks about half the size you expect, the template is working at 1080p: set it once on G1, confirm by eye against the frame, and use the same factor for the rest.

**Running time:** about 1:50, or 1:46 without the privacy line. If you need to cut more, trim the 0:10 practice list to three shots.

## Changes from the draft

- **Health moved into the free demo.** Linking to Health isn't Pro; only the extra Session Types are.
- **The trial is worded as "7-day free trial on the annual plan".** It no longer implies a limited-time offer.
- **The iPhone line is one beat with two halves:** starting on the Watch from your phone, and running on the phone alone. No haptics are promised on the phone.
- **"Rows, push-ups" became a plank,** the hands-busy exercise the alarm beat relies on.
- **"Blaring alarm" now appears on screen,** mid-plank, instead of only being said.
- **Added:** the step captions, the beat where the taps drop to silence, the paywall shot, the privacy line and the swappable end card.
