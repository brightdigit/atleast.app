# Shot List — all versions

Every shot the six scripts use, grouped so you can film each setup once. The **Used in** column shows which versions need a shot, where A/B/C are the full versions and a/b/c the 60s cuts. The **Account** column (Watch, Both devices and iPhone shots) says whether the app must be on a **Free** or **Pro** account, or **Any**; the talking-head, lifestyle and audio shots don't depend on it.

## Filming notes

- **Film the physical Watch and iPhone for anything shown working.** The rules want the app "functioning on the device". Film the Watch with a phone or camera in macro, and use the iPhone's built-in screen recording for iPhone screens. Use Simulator recordings only as extra cutaways, never as the main demo.
- **Why the split:** watchOS has no on-device screen recording (Xcode only takes stills), and the Watch beats sell a feeling on the wrist — the dim pulse, the motor, the silence — which a flat capture of a circle on black can't show. The iPhone beats are UI that has to be read (paywall plans, History stats), so a pixel-clean recording beats filming glass, and the phone never taps, so there's nothing physical to catch. Where the two meet (D1, D2), film both devices in one camera shot, with no separate screen recording cut in, so the handoff reads as real.
- **The pulse is dim by design,** so film W3 in a dark room with the camera exposed for the Watch face. Turn off Always On so the pulse isn't dimmed further.
- **Use a short timer for the silence and summary shots.** Film the UI with the **15 MIN** preset, the one from your story. Film the moment the taps stop and the **+X over** summary with the **1 MIN / 3s** preset, and hold about 15 seconds past the minimum so the summary reads "+0:15 over" or similar. No one has to sit for 15 minutes.
- **Set a Session Type for the summary:** set the Start-screen pill to **Mindfulness** before you press Start, so W5 shows "Saved as Mindful Minutes". Grant Health permission beforehand so the permission prompt doesn't appear in the shot.
- **Paywall shots (P5–P10):** use a **sandbox** account that isn't Pro. For the unlocked History shot (P8), use an account with a few days of real sessions, or the demo data behind the fastlane screenshots.
- **Film the Free shots first.** Shoot every **Free** shot (the W2 "Unlock on iPhone" take, P5–P7, P9, P10) before D2, because D2's sandbox purchase turns the account Pro. Then film P8 on Pro. To reshoot Free shots afterwards, use a fresh sandbox tester.
- **Don't record another app's alarm.** Make the L6 alarm sound yourself, or use a generic buzz you own, and don't show another app's UI.
